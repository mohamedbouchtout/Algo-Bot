# Risk Management

## Position Sizing

Position size is calculated in two steps, taking the smaller result:

1. **Risk-based sizing**: `shares = (account_value x risk_per_trade_pct) / abs(entry - stop)`
   - Ensures each trade risks exactly the configured percentage of your account
2. **Position cost cap**: `shares = (account_value x max_position_pct) / entry_price`
   - Prevents any single position from consuming too much capital (important for low-priced stocks with tight stops)

Example with a $100k account (`risk_per_trade_pct = 0.03`, `max_position_pct = 0.20`):

| Stock | Entry | Stop | Risk/Share | By Risk | By Cost Cap | Final |
|-------|-------|------|-----------|---------|-------------|-------|
| AAPL $180 | $180 | $175 | $5.00 | 600 shares | 111 shares | **111** |
| PENNY $0.50 | $0.50 | $0.43 | $0.07 | 42,857 shares | 40,000 shares | **40,000** |
| MSFT $420 | $420 | $410 | $10.00 | 300 shares | 47 shares | **47** |

## Built-in Safeguards

| Safeguard | Default | Description |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Risk per trade | 3% of account | Max equity risked on any single trade |
| Max investment | 70% of account | Remaining 30% stays in cash |
| Max positions | 10 | Limits concurrent open positions |
| Max position size | 20% of account | Caps any single position's cost |
| One position per stock | Always on | Prevents doubling down on the same ticker |
| Bracket orders | Always on | Every entry has a stop loss and take profit attached |
| Dynamic stop loss | VIX-adjusted | Wider stops in high-volatility environments |
| Risk/entry cap | 5% | Rejects any signal where risk exceeds 5% of entry price |

## Order Flow

1. Account snapshot is fetched once per scan cycle (not per signal)
2. Signal is generated by the strategy
3. `can_take_trade()` checks cash reserve and position count limits
4. `calculate_position_size()` computes shares using both formulas above
5. Trade cost is validated against available investment capacity
6. A bracket order is placed: market entry + GTC stop loss + GTC take profit
7. Bot waits up to 10 minutes for the entry to fill
8. If partially filled or unfilled, the entire bracket is cancelled and any partial fill is closed out

## Going Live

Before switching to real money:

1. Run on paper trading for at least 30 trading days
2. Track win rate (target >50%)
3. Monitor max drawdown (target <10%)
4. Manually verify signal quality in the logs
5. Test during volatile market conditions

When ready:

1. Switch to production branch: `git checkout bot/production`
2. Update `config/prod.json` with live account settings
3. Set IBKR port to `7496` (live TWS) or `4001` (live Gateway)
4. Consider reducing `risk_per_trade_pct` initially
5. Monitor closely for the first month
